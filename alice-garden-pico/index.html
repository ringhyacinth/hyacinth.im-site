Alice’s Garden

A talking garden in your room

Grow a talking garden on your coffee table.

GenreCozy MR gardening PlatformPICO MR + VR Pace10–20 minutes a day; no failure states InspirationLewis Carroll’s Alice in Wonderland

What it is

Cultivate a garden in your own room that opens into Alice’s world. Each flower has its own personality, and the garden you grow determines who visits. Tend it at your own pace, as in Stardew Valley or Pokémon Pokopia, with discoveries around every corner, as in The Legend of Zelda.

How to play Three simple actions

GrowPinch a seed and pull upward. A stem follows your hand, then blossoms and speaks.
Change sizeShrink like Alice and become a resident of your own garden.
WelcomeYour garden’s character attracts different visitors from the original story.

The room joins the story

Hold up a mirror

Point a real mirror at the garden. A new character climbs out of its reflection and leaps inside.

Tea at six

Open the game at 6 p.m. and find the Hatter and March Hare ready for tea beside your coffee table. Their clock is always stuck at six.

Turn out the lights

As your room darkens, night falls in the garden. The Cheshire Cat’s grin appears on your bookshelf, then vanishes in a blink.

Mirror and lighting interactions depend on real-world visual recognition, with available capabilities to be confirmed with PICO. A fallback would use mirror and window locations marked in a room scan.

Four moments in the garden Visual concepts

Pull a flower from a seed

The stem follows your hand. When the flower blooms, Tiger Lily begins to speak.

A score of dewdrops

Pluck the grass: dewdrops fall as musical notes, and the flowers sing the melody back.

A golden afternoon chorus

Conduct to the beat as the flowers sing, word by word, with golden light filtering through their leaves.

Walk around the garden

The garden is a 3D model. Walk around the coffee table and see it from every angle.
